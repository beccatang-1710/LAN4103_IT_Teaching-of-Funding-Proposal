Unit 5: Supplementary Information

单元五：补充资料

Task 1a — Addressing Concerns & Proposing Solutions

任务 1a — 处理关注事项及提出解决方案

When submitting funding applications, it is essential to address potential concerns that reviewers might have. By proactively acknowledging these concerns and presenting clear solutions, we demonstrate thorough evaluation and build confidence in our proposal's success.

提交资助申请时，必须处理审查员可能有的潜在关注事项。透过主动承认这些关注事项并提出明确的解决方案，我们展示了全面的评估，并建立对其提案成功的信心。

Sample: Supplementary Information

"We understand that there might be concerns about potential disruptions during the upgrade process. However, we will spare no effort in scheduling system updates during off-peak hours and conducting thorough testing, guaranteeing seamless operations for our clients."

「我们理解外界可能对升级过程中可能出现的干扰有所顾虑。我们将不遗余力地安排系统更新在非繁忙时段进行，并进行全面测试，确保客户运作不受影响。」

Analysis

What expressions are used to introduce the possible concern and solution?

  • Introducing the possible concern: "We understand that there might be concerns about..."
  • Introducing the solution: "However, we will spare no effort in..."

What effect does the phrase 'there might be concerns about' have on the tone?

This phrase introduces a potential issue in a tentative and polite manner. The word 'might' implies uncertainty, presenting the concern as a possibility rather than a confirmed problem. It softens the tone, avoiding sounding alarming.

What effect does the phrase 'we will spare no effort in...' have on the tone?

This phrase creates a tone of strong dedication and promise. The words 'will' and 'spare no effort' show firm commitment, making the message sound confident and certain, suggesting dedication to achieving the outcome.

Language Focus: Modals & Modifiers for Possibility and Certainty

Expressing Tentativeness (Possibility):
  • Modals: can, could, may, might
  • Modifiers: perhaps, probably, potentially, possibly, maybe, sometimes
  • Patterns:

— We understand/are aware that there might be concerns about...

— It is likely that certain issues could arise...

— Possible drawbacks might involve...

表达不确定性（可能性）：我们明白/意识到可能会有……的顾虑；某些问题可能会出现……；可能的缺点可能涉及……
Conveying Confidence & Commitment (Certainty):
  • Modals: will
  • Adverbs: absolutely, undoubtedly, definitely, certainly, clearly
  • Patterns:

— We will spare no effort in (+ gerund)

— We are committed to (+ gerund)

— We will take every measure to ensure that (+ clause)

— Please rest assured that...

传达信心及承诺（确定性）：我们将不遗余力地……；我们致力于……；我们将采取一切措施确保……；请放心……

Vocabulary: assertive (adj.) — expressing opinions strongly and with confidence; reassurance (n.) — something that is said to take away a person's fears or doubts

词汇：assertive（形容词）— 坚定自信地表达意见；reassurance（名词）— 消除他人恐惧或疑虑的话语

Task 2 — Ending a Funding Proposal Persuasively

任务 2 — 以具说服力的方式结束资助提案

After proposing solutions to potential concerns, you should end your proposal with a persuasive conclusion that reiterates the project's positive impact to leave a lasting impression on reviewers.

在提出潜在关注事项的解决方案后，你应以具说服力的结语结束提案，重申项目的正面影响，以给审查员留下深刻印象。

Sample Conclusion

"With the implementation of these robust cybersecurity measures to protect clients' data, we strongly believe that our service quality will be enhanced greatly, contributing to higher industry standards."

「随着这些强有力的网络安全措施的实施以保护客户数据，我们坚信服务质量将大幅提高，为行业更高标准作出贡献。」

Persuasive Concluding Expressions (+ clause)

  • We have no doubt that ...
  • We strongly / firmly believe that ...
  • We are confident / optimistic that ...
  • We trust that ...
  • It is believed that ...
具说服力的结语表达（+子句）：我们毫不怀疑……；我们坚信……；我们有信心……；我们相信……；据信……
